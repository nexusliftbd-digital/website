# 2️⃣ THE REPAIR LAYER (Operations & Workflows)
*This layer is about fixing, operating, and the day-to-day running of the company. When things break or need updates, this is the rulebook.*

### 🏢 Organogram (Company Structure)
For true delegation, everyone must know who reports to whom.
1. **CEO (Vision & System Architect):** Focuses ONLY on The Next & Vision layers, partnerships, and final Q/A.
2. **Operations Manager:** Connects the team, ensures SOPs are followed, handles escalated client issues.
3. **Development Team:** Frontend (Next.js/React), UI/UX Designers.
4. **Growth & Automation Team:** Make.com/Webhook experts, CRM managers.
5. **Sales & Support (Closers):** Handles incoming leads via FB/WhatsApp, follows the strict Sales Funnel.

### 🔄 Brand Guideline Connected to Sales Funnel
Brand identity is directly mapped to how a customer buys from us:
- **Top of Funnel (Awareness):** Ad creatives and FB posts use *Navy & Ice Blue*. Goal: Look premium and unapproachable by cheap competitors.
- **Middle of Funnel (Consideration - Website):** High-converting Conversion Website. The user sees the 'Nexus Business Health Monitor'. The UX is buttery smooth. They trust the *Emerald* CTA buttons.
- **Bottom of Funnel (Closing - Messenger/WhatsApp):** Closers do NOT beg for sales. Closers use consultative selling (e.g., "What is your current courier return rate? Let us fix that.").
- **Post-Purchase (Retention):** Automated Make.com onboarding messages. The premium experience must not drop after payment.

### ⚙️ Developer & Content SOP (Where to fix what)
If someone asks: *"Where are the products? How do I update a price?"*
- **SOP:** Open `src/data/products.ts`. All Product Titles, Prices, Descriptions, Options, and Statuses (`'live' | 'turned_off' | 'out_of_stock'`) live here. Update the JSON, push the code, and the entire site + CEO Dashboard updates instantly. 
- **CEO Dashboard (`/dashboard`):** Used to check live pipelines, lead statuses, without entering code.
